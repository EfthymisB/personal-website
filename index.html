            I'm Efthymios Bairaktaris, originally from Greece,
            now working in the Visual Effects industry as a Senior Pipeline
            Technical Director at
            One Of Us, London.
          

          

            As a Pipeline TD, I build the tools and workflows that keep visual
            effects production running. These are the systems artists rely on
            every day to do their best work without fighting the plumbing. It's a
            role that sits between the artists and the machines, and I've spent
            years making that space work smoothly at scale.
          

          

            Most of that work is software engineering: designing APIs and
            services, automating infrastructure, wrangling data across large
            productions, and lately folding AI and agentic tooling into the daily
            workflow. VFX is a demanding place to build software. Tight
            deadlines, huge datasets, and no room for things to break. The habits
            it teaches carry over to any team that needs solid, practical
            engineering, whatever they happen to be making.
